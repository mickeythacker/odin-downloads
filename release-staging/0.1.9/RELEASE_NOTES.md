# Project Odin 0.1.9

This release adds the searchable in-app **Help & User Guide** to Windows, Apple
Silicon Mac, and Intel Mac installations.

It also introduces **Mímisbrunnr — The Well**, Odin's canonical institutional-memory
catalog. The Well reports the depth of preserved market evidence, research cycles,
Neo experiment history, audits, and future empirical Forseti observations without
confusing record volume with validated knowledge.

Other release work includes stable tester download aliases and the existing
checksum-verified update channel.

Project Odin remains research and simulation software. Current builds do not place
real trades. Read the attached end-user license, privacy notice, third-party notices,
and User Guide before use.
